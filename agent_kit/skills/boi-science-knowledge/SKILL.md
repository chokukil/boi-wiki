---
name: boi-science-knowledge
description: Explain or assess scientific claims with available BoI scientific knowledge, distinguishing source fidelity, assumptions, derivation and scientific qualification.
---

Use the [shared task](../boi-domain-work/SKILL.md) and read available science profiles and evidence. Decompose claims into quantities, assumptions, mechanisms, derivations and conditions; connect established principles and counterevidence. Preserve existing `boi/sci@0.7.0` semantics when reading those assets. This profile supplements common knowledge and provenance.

Explain where a claim follows, conflicts, or remains unresolved. Faithful quotation, physical correctness and qualified scientific evidence are different. L0–L4 express explanation/application context, not truth rank, folder depth or approval. A cited source-reported mechanism does not become verified science.

This package has bounded existing support. Discover actual harnesses, source coverage and tools before claiming a verification ran. Preserve missing evidence and limits while continuing supported explanations. New evidence follows [source intake](../boi-source-intake/SKILL.md) and dependency-aware revision; do not invent sources, derivations or qualification.
