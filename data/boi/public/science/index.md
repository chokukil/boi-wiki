# Science Verifier knowledge

This tree contains agent-curated draft Science Source and Evidence objects for sci-profile 0.1. A Source record is provenance, not truth. Only a short, exact, locator-bound Evidence Span may support later Knowledge, and active release membership remains a separate gate.

## v0.1 boundaries

- General scientific reasoning, physics, chemistry, circuits, materials, semiconductor devices, and Spin Coating are represented.
- Emslie and Meyerhofer are retained as sources but their abstract-only spans are inactive until lawful full-text locator review is possible.
- The Merck AZ 125nXT spin-time sentence and page-10 figure observation are separate product- and revision-scoped drafts, not universal recipe rules.
- `evidence-claim-scope-v0.1.yaml` records what each span may and may not support; every mapping remains blocked from active release pending authorized Admin review.
- Paid Atkins and Agarwal/Lang books are bibliographic candidates only; no Source object or decision evidence is created without a lawfully accessible internal copy and exact locator.
